| Title        | Elections to be by secret ballot.                                                           |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First Added  | 14 August 1973                                                                              |
| Last Updated | 14 August 1973                                                                              |
| Source       | https://factfocus.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Original-Constitution-of-1973-Pakistan.pdf |

All elections under the Constitution, other than those of the Prime Minister and a Chief Minister, shall be by secret ballot.
