| Title        | Officers and servants of Courts.                                                            |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First Added  | 14 August 1973                                                                              |
| Last Updated | 14 August 1973                                                                              |
| Source       | https://factfocus.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Original-Constitution-of-1973-Pakistan.pdf |

The Supreme Court, with the approval of the President and a High Court, with the approval of the Gover- nor concerned, may make rules providing for the appoint- ment by the Court of officers and servants of the Court and for their terms and conditions of employment.
