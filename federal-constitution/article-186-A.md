| Title        | Power of Supreme Court to transfer cases |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| First Added  | 21 October 2024 |
| Last Updated | 21 October 2024 |
| Source       | https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/671f74b8da9e0_263.pdf |
| Amendment 26 | https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/671f74b8da9e0_263.pdf |

The Supreme Court may, if it considers it expedient to do so in the interest of justice, transfer any case, appeal or other proceedings, pending before any High Court to any other High Court or to itself.
