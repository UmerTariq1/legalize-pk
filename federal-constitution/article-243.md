| Title        | Command of Armed Forces.                                                                    |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| First Added  | 14 August 1973                                                                              |
| Last Updated | 04 April 1997                                                                               |
| Source       | https://factfocus.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Original-Constitution-of-1973-Pakistan.pdf |

(1) The Federal Government shall have control and command of the Armed Forces.

(2) The President shall subject to law, have power:—

(a) to raise and maintain the Military, Naval and Air Forces of Pakistan; and the Reserves of such Forces;

(b) to grant Commissions in such Forces; and

(c) to appoint the Chief of the Army Staff, the Chief of the Naval Staff and the Chief of the Air Staff, and determine their salaries and allowances.
